# Brew & Bean

## Project Description

Brew & Bean is a proposed coffee shop website designed to provide
customers with information about the coffee shop, its menu, products,
and contact details.

The website provides a simple and user-friendly way for customers to
explore the available drinks, bakery products and desserts offered by
Brew & Bean.

---

## Project Purpose

The purpose of the Brew & Bean website is to create an online presence
for the coffee shop and make important information easily accessible
to customers.

The website allows customers to:

- Learn about Brew & Bean.
- View the available menu items.
- Learn about the coffee shop's products and services.
- View relevant images.
- Find contact information.
- Send an enquiry through the contact form.

---

## Website Pages

The website consists of the following pages:

### Home

The Home page introduces Brew & Bean and provides an overview of the
coffee shop and its products.

### Menu

The Menu page displays the available food and drink categories,
including coffee, cold drinks, tea, bakery products and desserts.

### About Us

The About Us page provides information about Brew & Bean, including
its mission and values.

### Contact

The Contact page provides contact information and allows customers
to send an enquiry using the contact form.
